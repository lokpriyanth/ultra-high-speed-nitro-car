## What I am trying to achieve here:-
    A functional rc nitro car which has the capability to break the current rc nitro world record of 137kmph.

## How I plan on doing it:-
    I have narrowed down usage of the LRP NITRO ENGINE ZR.30 X COMPETITION engine for it's insane power to size ratio. I also plan on using all-metal parts(CNC-MACHINED) in places where I need it, like the gearbox, chassis, differential drive shaft etc... As 3d printed parts just can't handle the crazy amount of load while also spinning at a measly 36000 to 38000rpm.
